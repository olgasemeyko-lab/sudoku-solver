Tips

  • Arrow keys move between cells. Backspace or 0 empties a cell.
  • To paste a whole puzzle, click any cell and paste 81 characters, using a dot or 0 for empty cells.
  • A proper Sudoku has exactly one solution, and needs at least 17 given digits for that.

Read a puzzle from an image

Upload a screenshot or scan of a Sudoku. The app finds the grid, straightens it and cuts it into 81 cells. Reading the digits comes next; for now, type them into the grid above.

You can also drag an image onto this section, or paste one with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
