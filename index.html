Sudoku Solver

Take a photo or upload a screenshot of a Sudoku, or type its digits into the grid, then press Solve. Everything runs in your browser; your pictures aren't sent anywhere.

You can also drag a picture onto the page, or paste one with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).

Tips

  • Tap a cell, then a number below the grid. On a keyboard, type the digit; arrow keys move between cells and Backspace empties a cell.
  • To paste a whole puzzle, click any cell and paste 81 characters, using a dot or 0 for empty cells.
  • A proper Sudoku has exactly one solution, and needs at least 17 given digits for that.
